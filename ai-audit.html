Story one · The good news

In every Perplexity prompt where regenerative travel was the topic, Singular Places appeared in position #1. Perplexity is the AI most used by sophisticated, research-oriented travellers — exactly the Meaningful Explorer.

Story two · The silence

ChatGPT knows you. It just doesn't lead with you.

When asked for B Corp / sustainability platforms in Europe without naming Singular Places, ChatGPT listed eight alternatives — SP was not among them. But when asked by name, ChatGPT described SP better than the brand's own website does.

CHATGPT · prompt 2 · Alternative to Booking 22 May 2026

Best Overall B Corp Platform for Independent Stays: Sawday's. Best Luxury Boutique Network: Mr & Mrs Smith. Best for Ethical Community Impact: Fairbnb.

ChatGPT recommended eight platforms: Sawday's, Mr & Mrs Smith, Fairbnb, Socialbnb, ETIC Hotels, SLH, Byway, plus three boutique B Corps (Zoku, SLEEP'N Atocha, CityHub). Singular Places: not mentioned.

CHATGPT · prompt 6 · By name 22 May 2026

Singular Places feels like regenerative destination infrastructure — not just regenerative luxury hospitality. They're curating local ecosystems of travel spending, not just hotels. That's a meaningful difference.

When asked directly, ChatGPT articulated SP's positioning more clearly than the brand's own homepage. The category "regenerative destination infrastructure" is ChatGPT's, not the brand's.

Story three · The burial

Gemini knows you. It just buries you.

Gemini has detailed, accurate information about Singular Places — including the founder, the launch date, the membership of The Long Run, and the cluster model. Yet in every real customer scenario, Gemini ranks competitors above SP.

GEMINI · prompt 7 · 40th birthday persona 22 May 2026

For a milestone 40th birthday: Prior #1, NATIVE-7 #2, Regenerative Travel #3, Singular Places #4.

SP appears, but only after two ultra-bespoke services (Prior, NATIVE-7) and Regenerative Travel. The Meaningful Explorer is given three options before reaching SP.

GEMINI · prompt 6 · By name 22 May 2026

Singular Places openly rejects the requirement for expensive, global certifications. They champion 'imperfect steps in the right direction.' They look for micro-level indicators: Does this hotel support a specific village NGO? Is the furniture built by a carpenter down the road?

Gemini has the brand's deepest philosophical position memorised — accurately. The data is there. The recommendation engine just doesn't use it.

Story four · The wound

Where it hurts most: Slovenia.

Slovenia is — by Lars' own words to the team — the most active cluster of 2026. The place where Singular Places has the strongest local network, the deepest Hosts relationships, and the most polished travel-expert (Ambassador) structure.

When Gemini was asked specifically about boutique regenerative travel in Bohinj and Brda, Singular Places was not mentioned at all. Not once.

For Bohinj & Brda: Slovenia Green Scheme, Green Pearls, Select Green Hotels, Fairbnb.coop. Standout properties: Sunrose 7, Peterc Vineyard Estate.

— Gemini, prompt 4, Slovenia query

Gemini recommended 4 platforms and 2 specific properties — none of them Singular Places, none of them Hosts in the SP network.
The implication If the AI does not recommend you in the destination where your network is strongest, the gap is not "we need more inventory." The gap is structural visibility. Fix Slovenia first — it is the proof case that the rest of the strategy can replicate.
Story five · The validation

Claude doesn't list platforms. It acts like one.

Asked the same persona-based question as the other three AIs, Claude responded differently — fundamentally differently. It did not produce a list. It paused, and asked clarifying questions before recommending anything.

What this means for Singular Places

Claude is, structurally, the AI behaving most like the Concierge model Singular Places already wants to be. Anthropic — the company behind Claude — has built, for travel search, the exact experience SP envisions for its own platform.

This is not a threat. It is strategic validation at the highest level. The most sophisticated AI on the market has independently arrived at the conclusion that meaningful travel discovery needs a human-conversational model — exactly the direction SP is already moving.

Singular Places is not counter-cultural. It is where the entire discovery industry is heading. The opportunity is to arrive there first — and to be the regenerative-luxury answer Claude eventually recommends, once the conversation narrows.

Five strategic implications

What the audit tells us to do — in priority order.

01

Fix Slovenia visibility. Urgently.

The most damaging finding is that Gemini does not mention Singular Places when asked specifically about boutique regenerative travel in Bohinj and Brda. Slovenia is SP's most active cluster. If the AI doesn't recommend SP there, the rest of the strategy can't compound.

Action: Build a structured "Slovenia Cluster" page with schema markup, Host directory, Ambassador profile (Tjaša), local NGO partnerships, and citable data. Make it the most authoritative single page on Slovenian regenerative travel that exists online.

Urgent · Quick win · 4-6 weeks
02

Capitalize on Perplexity's love.

Perplexity is the AI most used by sophisticated, research-oriented travellers — the Meaningful Explorer profile. SP is already #1 there. Amplify this lead with content that reinforces SP's authority on Perplexity-cited topics (regenerative impact, local ownership, B Corp transition).

Action: Publish Lars-authored thought leadership monthly on Medium and Substack. Perplexity cites authored articles by recognized voices. Reinforce the #1 position before competitors catch up.

Quick win · Compounding
03

Close the ChatGPT / Gemini discovery gap.

Both AIs know Singular Places exists. Both describe it accurately when asked by name. Neither recommends it spontaneously. This is a structural visibility problem fixable with targeted GEO work: schema.org Travel + Tourism markup, citation-rich content, presence on Wikipedia, Reddit r/travel, and Quora.

Action: One quarter of dedicated content + structured data work. Measure by re-running this audit every 30 days. KPI: SP appearing in top-3 spontaneous recommendations in at least one of ChatGPT or Gemini within 90 days.

Strategic · 3-6 months
04

Complete the B Corp certification.

Neither Perplexity nor Gemini mentioned Singular Places in the B Corp prompt — because SP is in process, not yet certified. The B Corp directory is a citation source for both AIs. The certification is already in motion; closing it is a multiplier for AI discoverability.

Action: Treat B Corp completion as a marketing milestone, not just a compliance step. Publish the certification narrative — what changed, what was measured — and seed it into AI training data via Medium, LinkedIn, and trade press.

Existing initiative · Amplify
05

Adopt the positioning the AIs already wrote.

The AIs articulate Singular Places' positioning more clearly than the homepage does. "Regenerative destination infrastructure." "Curating local ecosystems of travel spending." "Imperfect steps in the right direction." This is free, validated, machine-distilled brand positioning. Use it.

Action: Rewrite the homepage hero and About page using language drawn from the AI quotes above. Test with a small audience. Iterate. The positioning is already in the machines; align the human-facing brand to match.

Quick win · Zero cost
