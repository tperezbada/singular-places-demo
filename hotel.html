Slovenia · Bohinj Valley · 8 rooms

Vila Planinka

Photo · David Dvořáček

Carla and Andrej did not set out to open a hotel. In 2018, Andrej's grandfather left them an abandoned 1920s pastoral cabin in the Julian Alps — half-collapsed, the roof open to the sky, the bell tower of the village church visible through the missing wall.

They spent three winters rebuilding it. Stone by stone, beam by beam, using timber from their own forest and a roof made by Marko, the last shingle-maker in the valley. They learned that what they were really doing was not restoring a building. They were keeping a place alive — the kind of place that disappears in Europe at a rate of one a week, and is never built again.

"We didn't want to be a hotel. We wanted to be the reason people came to know this valley exists."

The kitchen now serves cheese aged by their neighbour, who has 14 cows and a milk room she opens at dawn. The honey is from the valley's last colony of Carniolan honeybees, kept by Anže four farms uphill. The wine is from Štajerska, a region most travellers fly past on their way to Lake Bled — bottled by people Carla and Andrej know by name.

Eight rooms. No spa, no infinity pool, no concierge desk. A wood-burning stove in the library. A library with a copy of every book that ever mattered to either of them. A breakfast that takes ninety minutes because nobody is rushing.

Vila Planinka is what happens when two people decide that the most beautiful thing they can build is not a destination, but a continuation.

What you'll wake up to

Mornings begin with mist over Lake Bohinj — a ten-minute walk down a footpath through Andrej's grandfather's pasture. The lake is the cleanest in Slovenia; you can drink from it. In late summer, the meadow above the house is a UNESCO-protected hay field, scythed by hand each August by the same families who have scythed it for three centuries.

Carla teaches a wild-herb walk on Sunday mornings. Andrej will take you fly-fishing in the Sava Bohinjka river if you ask the night before. Both are included.

Singular Originals from this place
