How luxury travel actually works

Two booking models. One reaches the wealthy. One does not.

Most boutique platforms — including Singular Places today — operate on the left. The wealthy operate on the right. The gap is structural, not promotional.

— What SP does today
Direct booking model.
  1. Traveller discovers brand online (SEO, social, AI search)
  2. Traveller visits website, hesitates, compares
  3. Traveller books directly (or doesn't)
  4. No third-party trust signal, no perks, no problem-solving layer

"Excellent for the curious. Invisible to the truly wealthy."

✓ What the wealthy do
Advisor-routed booking.
  1. Wealthy traveller calls their personal travel advisor
  2. Advisor belongs to Virtuoso or Serandipians
  3. Advisor recommends and books from preferred suppliers
  4. Traveller receives upgrades, credits, perks — at no extra cost

"Built on trust. Routed through humans. Paid by the supplier, not the guest."

Founded 2013 by Quentin Desurmont because Europe lacked a US-style consortium. Capped at 600 members (plus a "Takumians" junior tier). Deliberately exclusive: two steering committees pre-select candidates, who then formally apply with business and growth strategy.

  • European-centric — perfect fit for SP's footprint
  • Smaller, peer-vetted, deeply curated
  • Hotel partners include Aman, Baglioni, SLH
  • By-invitation application — SP has a credible case

"Serandipians is the right first target. European-born, regenerative-aligned, smaller agencies that build deep relationships with HNW clients — and the founder explicitly says the network exists for companies like SP."

Why this matters

Three things a network membership gives Singular Places — all at once.

A.

A supplier-discount engine.

As a Serandipians partner, SP gains immediate visibility inside agencies' preferred-supplier directories. The travel advisor steers their client toward SP because it's how the model works — supplier pays commission, advisor earns, client gets perks. Zero marketing spend required.

B.

A peer-trust validator.

Membership is by invitation. The brand instantly becomes the "trusted supplier" tier — the same tier as Aman, Baglioni, SLH. SP stops being "new" and becomes "validated by the network the wealthy already trust."

C.

A direct pipeline to HNW clients.

790 agency members × an average book of HNW clients each = tens of thousands of qualified, repeat-booking travellers SP could not otherwise reach. Each advisor is a one-to-many sales channel. This is the channel solution Lars has been searching for.

What to do · 90 days

From "we should" to "we applied".

01

Map Serandipians' application requirements.

Identify the two steering committees, current SP-aligned member agencies, and the documentation needed: business case, growth strategy, host references, impact framework, B Corp progress.

Week 1–2
02

Position SP's application around the Cluster model.

Build the application narrative around what no other supplier in the network offers: regenerative cluster ecosystems, verified 86% local redistribution, The Long Run membership, and the 4Cs framework. The pitch is "the missing regenerative tier."

Week 3–4
03

Find two member-agency sponsors.

Approach EU agencies known for sustainability-aligned clients — particularly Spain, Germany, France. A sponsoring member dramatically improves application odds. Use Lars's network and Hosts' relationships to find them.

Week 5–8
04

Submit. Parallel-track Virtuoso research.

Submit Serandipians application by week 12. In parallel, begin mapping Virtuoso's by-invitation pathway for year-two consideration. The two are complementary, not competitive.

Week 9–12

One change. Everything follows.

Singular Places does not need a bigger marketing budget. It needs to enter the room where the wealthy are already buying. Serandipians is that room.
