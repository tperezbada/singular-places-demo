Idea one · Distribution

Serandipians Network Membership.

Lars's Explorer books through a travel advisor, not a website. Serandipians is a by-invitation European network of 790 luxury travel agencies representing €6 billion in annual sales — and their clients are exactly Lars's target. Joining as a supply partner puts Singular Places inside the advisor relationship without a single euro of ad spend.

For Lars

"Your Explorer listens to their travel designer. The 790 agencies inside Serandipians are those designers. Right now none of them have a structural reason to recommend you. This fixes that."

Idea two · Press

Editorial PR, anchored to the B Corp moment.

Five years of operation. Zero mainstream press. The story — a founder with 25 years in boutique hotels building a platform where 86% of every booking stays locally, now B Corp certified — is a feature article waiting to happen. One well-placed piece reaches the Explorer directly, gives advisors something to cite, and feeds AI discovery tools simultaneously.

For Lars

"B Corp certification is the best press trigger you will ever have. Most companies put the badge in the footer. You should treat it as a brand launch."

Idea three · Positioning

The Anti-OTA Manifesto.

The website currently speaks to two audiences at once — the values-aligned HNW Explorer and a mainstream traveller being educated about sustainability — and lands clearly with neither. A short, declarative manifesto tells the visitor in ten seconds what Singular Places is, who it is for, and what it stands against. It is what Lars communicates naturally in conversation but the homepage does not yet say.

For Lars

"Your website confused our team on first visit — and we had already spoken to you. Your Explorer has ten seconds to decide if this is their platform."

Idea four · Product

Productised Named Experiences.

Instead of 99 map pins requiring self-assembly, create 6 to 8 named, bookable journeys with a feeling at their centre. Each bundles Stay, Taste, Discover, and Local Expert into one emotionally resonant package. Named experiences are easy for advisors to recommend, easy for press to write about, and easy for Lars to pitch at ITB.

For Lars

"Black Tomato's 'Get Lost' generated press for a decade from one creative decision. You have the inventory to do the same."

Idea five · Sales force

Visible & Incentivised Travel Experts.

Two moves that only work together. First, make Travel Experts visible — named profiles, photographs, the properties they personally curate. Second, give them a financial reason to sell: a commission share on bookings they originate. Right now they contribute knowledge but have no incentive to bring their own clients. Ten country experts with HNW networks become Singular Places' lowest-cost sales team.

For Lars

"Every successful luxury house leads with its people. Your Travel Experts exist but are invisible. Giving them a commission share turns them into your sales force."

Idea six · Experience

FAM Trips, after network membership.

The product is felt, not explained. A familiarisation trip puts 6 to 10 Serandipians advisors inside one Cluster. The Host absorbs the stay, the experience partners absorb the activities, Singular Places contributes coordination. In return, six advisors who have lived the product carry it into their HNW client books for years. Highest ROI channel available, near-zero cash cost.

For Lars

"You said the product is hard to explain. The solution is not better copy — it is putting the right people inside a Cluster and letting the place do the talking."

Idea seven · Voice

The Anti-Bucket-List Campaign.

Virtuoso's own 2026 data shows avoiding overtourism is the top priority for wealthy travellers. Singular Places' entire inventory is places those travellers have not heard of. That is not a weakness — it is the positioning. "We do not do the bucket list. We do the places the bucket list forgot." Homepage statement, press angle, newsletter series, social voice. Cost: almost nothing.

For Lars

"Your Explorer does not want to go where everyone else goes. Your inventory is built for exactly that. Say it directly."

Idea eight · Geography

Spain as the Flagship Market.

Singular Places is present in 28 countries but dominant in none. Spain has the largest supply concentration, a government explicitly pivoting to quality-over-quantity tourism, gastronomic tourism up 28%, and Lars physically based in Madrid. Go deepest here first — the strongest cluster network, the first named experiences, the first FAM trips, the first press story — prove the model completely, then template it globally.

For Lars

"What if instead of a thin global platform, you became the definitive regenerative travel platform for Spain first — and used that as the proof of concept for everywhere else?"
