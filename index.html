The problem we found

Two customers. One brand.

Singular Places signals two messages at once. One promises upscale luxury for the intellectually curious Meaningful Explorer. The other promises regenerative Clusters where 86% of every booking stays in the community. These describe two different customers — and pursuing both at once is what makes the brand feel undefined today.

01

The luxury chase is asymmetric.

Mr&Mrs Smith, Tablet/Michelin Keys, and Black Tomato have decades of capital, catalogue and brand. Competing on those terms means winning on a field they own.

02

The regenerative field is open.

Almost no direct European competition. Regenerative Travel (US) plays the same space, but their perfectionist threshold caps their supply. SP's imperfect-steps philosophy is the moat — not a limitation.

03

The strongest asset is buried.

SP's most defensible differentiator is the Cluster model — independent Hosts grouped into regenerative ecosystems with shared NGO funding. No competitor has it. Today it sits buried below booking buttons.

Our proposal · three movements

Built in sequence, not in parallel. Each pays for the next.

I.
Rewrite the narrative.
Same CMS, different conversation. Every hotel becomes a story. Sub-sites per destination. The 86% of every booking that stays in the community becomes the headline message — not the booking buttons. No new platform required.
0–6 months Zero capex
II.
Launch the WhatsApp Concierge.
Six Singular Originals arranged by humans, not by web. You aggregate your travel experts — they keep their tour-operator licence; you keep the brand. Commission rises from 14% to 25–30% on this line.
6–12 months One FTE
III.
Open the community by invitation.
Annual membership €1,500–2,500. B2B alliances with brands of compatible DNA: Patagonia, Aesop, Veja, family offices with ESG criteria. Recurring revenue, pre-qualified customers.
12–24 months Recurring revenue
How it works · From first message to Impact Receipt

Five steps. One human. Zero forms.

No search bar. No filters. No "best price guarantee". Just a conversation that ends with a trip — and a transparent receipt showing exactly where your money went.

I

You write.

One WhatsApp message: who you are, when, what kind of trip. Ninety seconds.

II

A Concierge reads you.

A human (not a bot) reviews your brief and routes it to the local Ambassador of your destination.

III

Your Ambassador designs the route.

Hotels + experiences + every Host called personally to confirm. Within 24 hours.

IV

You receive one proposal.

Day by day. With the 86% breakdown — exactly where your money goes. Yes or no.

V

After the trip, an Impact Receipt.

What your money funded: a season of conservation, an apprentice's salary, a craft kept alive.

"On Booking, this is impossible. On the hotel website, it is impossible. Here, it is the product."

Concept · six originals the filter could produce

Six concept experiences.

These six are concept proposals, not current SP inventory. The intent is to show what the model produces when the filter is applied to SP's existing geography and Host network — not to claim what already exists.

Each would follow the same logic: hosted by someone in SP's network, paying a craft or community on the edge of disappearing, and impossible to book anywhere else online. That is what makes an experience Singular.

Filter: pays a disappearing craft · keeps cultural heritage · supports a small community.

01
Slovenia · BohinjWild honey morning
A morning with Anže, 4th-generation keeper of the endangered Carniolan bee.
Funds one season of bee conservation
02

Funds heritage textile workshop

05
