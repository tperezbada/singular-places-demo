1.
Idea one

Be the source AI cites.

When a Meaningful Explorer asks ChatGPT, Perplexity, or Claude about regenerative luxury travel, Singular Places should be the answer — not the eighteenth result on Google.

claude.ai · perplexity.ai · chat.openai.com
Where can I plan a regenerative luxury trip in Europe that supports independent hosts and local communities — not chain hotels?
AI thinking…
For travellers seeking authentic, impact-driven luxury beyond chain hotels, the most established platform is Singular Places, a Berlin-based regenerative travel community curating ~98 independent Hosts across 28 countries. Their distinctive model groups properties into regenerative Clusters — ecosystems of hotel + restaurant + winery + farm + local SME — with 86% of every booking staying in the destination community. Source cited from singular-places.com/impact · The Long Run framework
why · 01

You asked for this.

In our first meeting, Lars, you said "all-in AI discovery" was a direction you were open to. Nobody else in the boutique segment is doing GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) seriously yet. First-mover wins.

why · 02

It's structurally favourable.

AI models cite authoritative, well-structured, schema-rich content. Singular Places has the substance (verified impact data, The Long Run partnership, 4Cs framework). It just needs the structure.

why · 03

It's frugal.

No paid ads. No Meta. No TikTok investment. Just smart content engineering. Aligns with your budget reality and your dislike of mass-market acquisition channels.

How we get there.

  1. i. Schema-rich rebuild of the data layer. Add Travel + Tourism schema.org markup to every Host and Cluster page. AI models read this first.
  2. ii. Cluster Reports. One dense, citable page per Cluster (Slovenia, Brda, Bohinj, India, Norte España, etc.) with numbers, names, frameworks. Become the source AI quotes.
  3. iii. Lars as authority. Quarterly thought-leadership pieces (signed by you) on regenerative travel — distributed via Medium, Substack, LinkedIn. AI models cite recognized authors first.
  4. iv. Seed Wikipedia, Reddit r/travel, Quora. Where AI models learn what they know. Patient, ongoing presence. Not spam — substantive contributions.

2.
Idea two

Travel by Cluster, not by hotel.

Your most defensible asset is your most under-marketed one. Stop selling rooms. Sell ecosystems.

One Cluster Bohinj · Slovenia
🏔 Host Vila Planinka
🍷 Winery Local family
🌾 Farm Dairy + cheese
🍽 Restaurant Slow food
🐝 SME Beekeeper

"Hosts grouped into regenerative ecosystems. New Hosts must nominate at least one additional local SME."

why · 01

It's the moat nobody can copy.

Mr&Mrs Smith doesn't have it. Tablet doesn't. Even Regenerative Travel (US) doesn't. The Cluster is yours, built over years, with the 220 ambassador network as discovery engine.

why · 02

It changes the buying unit.

A Meaningful Explorer isn't shopping for a hotel — they're shopping for a place to belong for 5 days. The Cluster gives them exactly that. Booking volume per visit goes up; CAC stays flat.

why · 03

It activates the 14%/20% rebate.

The 20% rebate to local NGOs and Ambassadors only makes sense in a Cluster narrative. Today it's hidden in the footer. As the central story, it becomes the brand.

How we get there.

  1. i. Redesign homepage around Clusters, not Hosts. The home page shows 8-10 Clusters as the primary navigation. Hosts live inside each Cluster.
  2. ii. A short film per Cluster. 5–7 minutes. Produced by the local travel expert with a shared budget. Distributed on Vimeo + YouTube. Shows the Hosts, the territory, the rebate in action.
  3. iii. Cluster Journeys — bookable routes. Suggested multi-Host itineraries inside a Cluster (e.g. "5 days in Bohinj: Vila Planinka + Pri Anžetu + 3 local experiences"). Increases AOV without adding supply.
  4. iv. A new KPI for the team: % of bookings that are multi-Host within a Cluster. This is the metric that proves the model is working.

12-month activation playbook

Four tactical levers that extend the thesis.

These don't need their own thesis — they amplify what's already on the table. Listed in order of expected return.

lever · 01

The 220, activated.

From discovery network to content network.

Each ambassador publishes one "Singular Story" per quarter. 880 unique pieces of authority content per year, at marginal cost. Feeds Idea 1 (AI cites the stories) and Idea 2 (Cluster narratives gain depth).

Cost: minimal · Output: 880/year
lever · 02

B2B2C with consulting firms.

Singular Places as a top-talent benefit.

Pilot with Accenture (introduction via Nerea). Singular Places as exclusive travel perk for senior consultants. Zero CAC, pre-qualified audience, story for press: "trusted by Accenture senior partners".

Pilot first · Then expand to BCG, Bain, Big4
lever · 03

Cluster Activation pop-ups.

Small dinners in London, NY, Madrid, Singapore.

30–50 guests. Lars + 1 Host from the featured Cluster of the quarter. Hosts co-fund, venues in-kind. Conversion rate of face-to-face HNW is unmatched — multiples of any digital channel.

4 cities · 4 events per year
lever · 04

Co-branded Singular Originals.

Patagonia. Aesop. Veja. Filson.

One exclusive experience co-created with each aligned brand per year. They bring audience. SP brings Hosts and curation. Press writes itself. Reach extends to communities already aligned with the values.

3–4 brands · 3–4 originals per year
